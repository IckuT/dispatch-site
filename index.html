Dispatch

A personal publishing tool

Dispatch is a private tool its owner uses to schedule and publish their own photos and videos to their own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It runs on the owner's computer and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Lets the owner write a post once and choose which of their own accounts it goes to.
  • Publishes at the chosen time using each platform's official API.
  • Shows which posts went out and which need attention.
