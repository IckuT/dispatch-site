Dispatch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Dispatch is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who connects their own social media accounts to publish their own content. This page explains what information Dispatch handles.

Information Dispatch receives

When the owner connects an account, the platform (Instagram, TikTok or YouTube) gives Dispatch:

  • Basic profile information: account name, username and profile picture.
  • Access tokens that let Dispatch publish on the owner's behalf.
  • Results of publishing, such as the link to a published post, and basic post statistics.

How it is used

This information is used only to show which account a post goes to, to publish the owner's posts, and to show the owner how their posts performed. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on the owner's own computer or private server. Photos and videos are sent only to the platform they are being published to.

Removing access and data

The owner can remove an account from Dispatch at any time, which deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked from each platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). Disconnecting stops Dispatch from receiving any further information.
